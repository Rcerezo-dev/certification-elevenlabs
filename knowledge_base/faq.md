# NovaMart Frequently Asked Questions

## About NovaMart
**What is NovaMart?**
NovaMart is an online retailer selling Nova-brand electronics and home goods. NovaMart has no physical stores; all orders are placed online at novamart.example.

## Customer support hours
**When can I reach customer support?**
- Phone support: Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern Time; Saturday and Sunday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern Time.
- The automated phone assistant is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Transfers to a human agent are only possible during phone support hours.
- Closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day.
- The website accepts orders 24 hours a day.

## Shipping
**Where do you ship?**
To all 50 US states. NovaMart does not ship internationally or to PO boxes.

**How much does shipping cost and how long does it take?**
| Option | Cost | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Free on orders of $50 or more; $5.99 otherwise | 5 to 7 business days |
| Expedited | $14.99 | 2 to 3 business days |
| Next day | $24.99 | Next business day if ordered by 1 p.m. Eastern Time |
Deliveries to Alaska and Hawaii take 2 to 4 extra business days. Next-day shipping is not available there.

**What do the order statuses mean?**
- **Processing:** the order has been placed and is being prepared. The shipping address can still be changed.
- **Shipped:** the order has left the warehouse. The shipping address can no longer be changed.
- **Delivered:** the carrier has confirmed delivery.

**Can I change my shipping address?**
Yes, but only while the order is still **processing**, and only after we verify your identity with your account number and postcode. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.

**Can I cancel my order?**
Cancellations are handled by a human support agent while the order is still processing. Once shipped, the order can be returned instead under the returns policy.

**My tracking says delivered but I don't have my package.**
Wait 24 hours, as carriers sometimes mark packages early, and check with neighbors. If it still has not arrived, a human support agent can open a carrier investigation.

## Warranty
**What warranty do Nova products have?**
- All Nova products have a **1-year limited warranty** from the delivery date.
- The **Nova Gaming Laptop** has a **2-year limited warranty**.
- The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, normal wear, or damage from unauthorized repairs.
- Warranty claims are handled by a human support agent, who will need the order number.

**Do you sell extended warranties?**
No. NovaMart does not currently sell extended warranties or protection plans.

## Payment
**Which payment methods do you accept?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and NovaMart gift cards.
NovaMart does not accept cash, checks, or cryptocurrency.

**When is my card charged?**
When the order is placed.

**Do you offer installments?**
Orders of $200 or more can be paid in 4 interest-free installments through PayPal at checkout.

**Do you price match?**
No. NovaMart does not price match other retailers.

**Can I use more than one gift card?**
Yes, up to 3 gift cards per order, combined with one other payment method.

## Account and security
**How do you verify my identity on the phone?**
We ask for your 8-digit account number and the postcode on your account. We never ask for your password or full card number over the phone.

**Where do I find my account number?**
In the "My Account" section of novamart.example and at the top of every order confirmation email.
