# NovaMart Returns Policy

## The basics
- **Return window:** 30 days from the delivery date.
- **Proof of purchase required:** the order number or the receipt (order confirmation email) is required for every return.
- **Refund method:** refunds go back to the original payment method. Gift card purchases are refunded to a NovaMart gift card.
- **Refund timing:** refunds are issued within 5 business days after the return arrives at the warehouse. Banks may take 3 to 5 more business days to show it.

## What can be returned
- **Unopened items** in their original sealed packaging, from any category, within 30 days.
- **Home Goods** (robot vacuum, air purifier, smart kettle, desk lamp) may be returned **even if opened**, as long as they are in like-new condition with all parts and the original box, within 30 days.

## What cannot be returned
- **Opened electronics.** Once the factory seal on an electronics product is broken, it cannot be returned for a refund. This applies to: Nova Headphones X1, Nova Smart Speaker Mini, Nova Controller for Nintendo Switch, Nova 3D Printer, Nova Gaming Laptop, and Nova Scanner.
- Items returned after 30 days from delivery.
- Items without an order number or receipt.
- Used consumables, such as opened 3D printer filament or used air purifier filters.
- Gift cards.

## Defective or damaged items
- An item that arrives **damaged or not working** is not a normal return. It can be replaced under warranty, even if the product is opened electronics.
- Damage in shipping must be reported within **7 days** of delivery.
- Defects after that are covered by the product warranty: 1 year for all Nova products, 2 years for the Nova Gaming Laptop.
- Defective, damaged, or wrong-item cases are handled by a human support agent. The voice agent should offer a transfer.

## Return shipping
- **Free return shipping** when the item is defective, damaged, or NovaMart sent the wrong item.
- For other returns (changed mind, wrong size, no longer needed), a **$7.99 return shipping fee** is deducted from the refund.

## How to start a return
1. Confirm the item is eligible: within 30 days, with the order number, and not opened electronics.
2. The customer starts the return online at novamart.example/returns using the order number, **or** asks to be connected with a human support agent who can start it for them.
3. The customer receives a prepaid return label by email.
4. The customer packs the item in its original packaging and drops it off at any carrier location within 14 days of receiving the label.

## Exchanges
NovaMart does not do direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

## Rules for the agent
- Do not promise exceptions to this policy. If a customer asks for an exception, offer to transfer them to a human agent.
- Do not invent restocking fees, deadlines, or conditions that are not written here.
